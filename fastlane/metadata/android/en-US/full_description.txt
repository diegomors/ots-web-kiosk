<p><b>OTS Kiosk</b> is a minimal full-screen web kiosk designed for Android TV, Google TV, touch displays, mouse, and keyboard.</p><p><br><b>Features</b></p><ul><li>Immersive WebView with JavaScript, storage, cookies, redirects, and media playback</li><li>Touch interaction remains enabled on TV devices</li><li>Real Android display rotation at 0°, 90°, 180°, and 270° keeps page, touch input, and keyboard aligned</li><li>Automatic recovery after network and WebView renderer failures</li><li>Web geolocation for the configured trusted HTTPS origin</li><li>Optional startup after device boot</li><li>Optional best-effort foreground recovery</li><li>PIN-protected settings and kiosk exit</li></ul><p>Hold the Back button for approximately two seconds to open Admin Access. A short Back press is ignored.</p>
